1. About this policy

Wallpaper Master is a live wallpaper app. It lets you pick a design, customize it, and use it as your home screen or lock screen wallpaper. Some designs show your own events, birthdays, daily schedule or goals.

This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app uses, why, where it stays, and what choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean Gopal Software, the developer of the app.

2. What the app uses

All of the information below is created on, or read on, your device. None of it is uploaded to our servers, because the app has no servers and no internet permission.

The festival dates and daily quotes in the app are built into the app itself. The app does not download them.

3. What we don't collect

The app does not collect, and does not ask for:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or home address
  • An account, password or sign-in
  • Your location (including GPS)
  • Your contacts, messages or call history
  • Your photos, videos, files, camera or microphone
  • Your advertising ID, device identifiers or browsing history
  • Payment or financial information

The app contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking tools.

4. Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhat it is forIf you say no

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The app does not include any third-party advertising, analytics or tracking services, so there is no one for your information to be passed to.

A few buttons let you start an action yourself. When you tap them, the app hands over to another app, and that app's own privacy policy applies:

  • Share the app opens your phone's share sheet. Only a link and message about the app are shared.
  • Email support opens your email app. You decide what to write and whether to send.
  • Help group link may open Telegram, if you choose to join.
  • Share a crash report lets you send a crash report through an app you pick. Nothing is sent unless you do this.

If a law ever required us to disclose information, we would have nothing to give, because we do not hold any of your information.

Google Play itself may collect information when you download or update the app. That is covered by Google's own privacy policy, not this one.

6. Storage and security

Everything the app stores sits in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally read. Because the app does not send data over the internet, your information is not exposed in transit.

Phone backups. If you have turned on Android backup for your phone, Google may include app data (such as your settings and events) in your device backup in your own Google account. That is controlled by your phone's backup settings and Google's policies, not by us. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we also suggest keeping a screen lock on your phone.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your information stays on your phone until you remove it. You can delete it any time:

  • Events, birthdays, schedule and goals: delete them inside the app.
  • Everything the app stored: go to Settings → Apps → Wallpaper Master → Storage → Clear data. This also removes saved crash reports.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its data from your phone.
  • Your phone's calendar events are not copied by the app. They stay in your calendar app, where you manage them.

Because we do not keep any of your information on our side, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

8. Children

Wallpaper Master is a general-purpose app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us (see section 11).

9. Your choices and rights

You are in control of your information on your device. You can:

  • Allow or turn off calendar and notification access at any time
  • View, change and delete everything you entered into the app
  • Clear all app data or uninstall the app

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (European Union and UK), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and California's CCPA may give you rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. Since we do not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing to request from us. If you still have a question or concern, we are happy to help.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If the app ever adds features that handle information differently, such as online features, we will update this policy and our Google Play data safety information before those features are released.
