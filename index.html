Privacy Policy — Wallpaper Master

Developer: Gopal Software Contact: kishanyadavgky@gmail.com Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Wallpaper Master app ("the app") handles information. The short version: the app does not collect, upload, sell or share your personal data.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data from your device to us or to anyone else. The app does not contain advertising or analytics/tracking services.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves your choices on your device only (in the app's private storage):

  • the wallpaper design you picked, plus text, colors, fonts, sizes and positions you customize;
  • content you type into the app, such as names, goals, time-table entries and your own events;
  • app settings such as language, theme and notification preferences.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR), optional: if you allow it, the app reads event titles and dates from your device calendar so it can show festivals and events on your wallpaper. This is read on your device only; calendar data is not stored on any server or shared.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), optional: used to show reminders and event notifications that you enable.
  • Live wallpaper service: required by Android so the app can draw your wallpaper.

You can turn any of these off at any time in Android Settings. The app still works without them, with fewer features.

4. Crash reports

If the app crashes, it may save a short error report (technical details such as the error and device model) as a text file on your device. It is not sent anywhere automatically. It leaves your device only if you choose to share or copy it yourself, for example to email it to us for support.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not share your data with anyone because we do not receive it. The app uses standard open-source Android libraries to run; they do not collect personal information through the app. If you tap a link to open Google Play or send us an email, that action is handled by the other app you open, under its own privacy policy.

6. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Security

Your settings stay in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but since nothing is transmitted by the app, the risk is limited to your own device's security.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change (for example, if features that use the Internet are added), we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before releasing that version.
